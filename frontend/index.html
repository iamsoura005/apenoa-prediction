Biomedical Engineering Context

PPG-Based Sleep Apnea Predictive Forewarning

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) is characterized by recurrent upper airway collapse during sleep, resulting in hypoxia, severe sleep fragmentation, and acute autonomic nervous system stress. Conventional diagnostics require overnight polysomnography (PSG) in a hospital sleep lab with 16+ wired channels.

Autonomic Modulation in PPG

Photoplethysmography measures changes in microvascular blood volume. As an airway collapse approaches, the body's autonomic sympathetic system reacts prior to complete cessation of airflow:

  • Peripheral Vasoconstriction: Causes progressive pulse amplitude drop.
  • Mayer Wave Fluctuations: 0.1 Hz oscillations in arterial blood pressure.
  • Pulse Transit Time (PTT) Variation: Surges with sympathetic tone.
  • Heart Rate Variability (HRV) Elevation: Spectral power shifts to Low Frequency (LF).

Why Predict Instead of Detect?

Conventional systems detect apnea after or during its occurrence (when blood oxygen SpO₂ has already crashed). By leveraging a 5-second lead time, ApneaSphere forecasts impending events before airway obstruction solidifies:

  • Enables proactive auto-titrating CPAP pressure pre-emptive adjustment.
  • Facilitates closed-loop vibratory or acoustic positional therapy.
  • Eliminates oxygen desaturation troughs before they harm cardiac tissue.
